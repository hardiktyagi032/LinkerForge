Status: active implementation program, not a demonstrated improvement. The one-time public CAID3 common-27 retrospective is spent; its scores must never select a new architecture or threshold. The new [untouched-comparison contract](DFL_UNTOUCHED_COMPARISON_CONTRACT_2026-10-17.md) supersedes the old split's training eligibility: the current split overlaps candidate DP94 and its preflight fails closed. Rebuild and certify benchmark-excluded, family-audited data before any new training. Keep DFL separate from DLD IDL/DDL labels.

## What published methods have that our frozen ESM2-35M SAM head lacks

1. **Complementary evolutionary and larger-pLM representations.** [LINKER-Pred2 documentation](https://pcrgwd.ucd.ie/linker_pred/help/) describes ProtTrans plus MSA-Transformer embeddings and an ensemble CNN; its Lite version omits MSA. Implement a versioned optional feature-fusion interface that refuses missing per-residue embeddings, sequence mismatches, or embeddings computed from protected labels. First test a matched richer-encoder baseline, then MSA features only where both arms have equal coverage and compute. The local 6 GB GPU may require offline/cloud extraction. This is not a claim that either input caused its CAID3 advantage.
2. **Disorder-to-DFL specialization.** [TransDFL](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10626177/) pretrains an IDR predictor, then fine-tunes for DFL with CNN/BiLSTM sequence labeling. Our auxiliary IDR transfer exists, but our final DFL head does not explicitly represent an IDR propensity or disorder/ordered distinction. Implement a separate predicted-disorder channel/gate using only training-source IDR labels; compare to the existing matched transfer baseline. Do not feed ground-truth disorder or CAID3 labels at inference.
3. **Whole-protein and putative-structure context.** [APOD](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7773485/) uses local plus protein-level disorder content, conservation, predicted secondary structure and solvent accessibility. Implement a masked global-context branch and, separately, versioned sequence-derived structure/conservation inputs. Any predicted feature must be generated independently of the target label and available for every compared target. Never use native cross-domain geometry or test annotations as input.
4. **Long-range sequence and explicit span behavior.** [TransDFL](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10626177/) combines BiLSTM and CNN for local/long-range context, while our SAM head has query spans but the reported primary score is the semantic residue channel and its region recovery is weak. Implement an ablation of a local/dilated sequence branch plus a coherent span decoder; compare residue APS, Fmax, high-precision recall, region IoU, boundary error, runtime, and no-positive proxy. Prior bounded-span variants were negative, so do not assume a span module helps.
5. **Multitask function relations and curated source mixing.** [DisoFLAG](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10762911/) uses pLM features, recurrent context and a function-interaction graph; [LINKER-Pred2 documentation](https://pcrgwd.ucd.ie/linker_pred/help/) describes DLD plus DisProt training. Implement source-specific masked heads and a small function-interaction module only when ontology-aligned, provenance-bearing auxiliary labels exist; do not relabel DLD structural linkers as DFL or treat missing annotations as negatives. Compare against same-data single-task heads. This arm is **data-gated** until label mapping and family protection pass.

These are five implementable work packages, not five validated model gains. The first executable code round should isolate mechanisms 2-4 on existing protected data; packages 1 and 5 require additional inputs/annotation audits. Every change needs a matched baseline, fixed seeds, source/data/config hashes, bounded GPU trial, and negative-result ledger. Do not combine mechanisms until single-mechanism gains survive three seeds and guardrails.

## Three communicating schedules

- **Transfer diagnosis:** CPU-first audits of label semantics, source shift, score calibration, family overlap, windowing, and errors. Write append-only dated findings under `reports/track-transfer/`; read the other two tracks' latest notes before proposing a test. No CAID3 per-protein or label reuse for model selection.
- **Competitor mechanisms:** Verify primary sources, obtainable code/weights/data, input modality, compute, and fair matched ablations. Write dated findings under `reports/track-competitors/`; feed actionable, non-speculative mechanisms to the implementation track. Do not infer the private IPA-AF2-Linker architecture from its name or leaderboard score.
- **General-ML implementation:** Build fail-closed feature/head variants and bounded matched trials on protected development only, drawing from partial-label sequence learning, precision constraints, and segmentation methods. Write dated plans/results under `reports/track-ml/`; inspect transfer and competitor findings before each new round. One local GPU job globally, never one job per schedule.

Each schedule reads this program, the other track directories, `reports/DFL_SAM_REPEAT_AND_LOCKED_CAID3_2026-10-17.md`, and the current GPU/process ledger before acting. Each writes only to its own track directory and uses uniquely named outputs. A schedule must produce one concrete artifact or reconcile a real job; unchanged state is not a reason to manufacture a training run. Research runs continue until the user stops them, in individually bounded rounds of <=3 architecture variants and <=6 GPU-hours, with at least three seeds for promising arms. Never auto-promote, commit, push, overwrite unrelated work, or claim frontier performance without an untouched common-target test.

## Why transfer was poor: current evidence boundary

The frozen baseline scored much better on its filtered development set than on the public CAID3 common-27 set. This establishes **distribution/benchmark transfer failure**, not a unique cause. Candidate mechanisms include label-policy differences, tiny positive and no-positive samples, source/family shift, missing evolutionary/structural context, and architectural overfit. Disambiguate with controlled source-matched and family-stratified audits; do not change an architecture merely because it improved a previously viewed CAID3 score.
