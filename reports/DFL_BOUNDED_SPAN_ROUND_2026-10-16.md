## Data/representation diagnostic after the two results

The screened train file has 79 proteins and 34,445 residues: 2,272 positive, 7,695 published negative, and 24,478 unknown/masked (`x`). The screened development file has 42 proteins and 22,968 residues: 1,267 positive, 6,160 negative, and 15,541 unknown. Unknown residues were masked during training/scoring, not converted to negatives.

The current candidate-span maximum is 64 residues. Direct counts of contiguous positive regions in the **full source labels** show 9/61 training spans are longer than 64 (952/2,272 positive residues) and 6/30 development spans are longer than 64 (610/1,267 positive residues). Thus the candidate scorer cannot represent roughly 42% of training positive residues or 48% of development positive residues as whole intervals. Windowing can change the exact number of evaluated regions (the trial evaluator reports 28 development gold regions); the full-label counts and evaluator counts must not be conflated. This is a verified representation ceiling, **not proof** that it caused the entire APS deficit. A third data-driven variant may increase the span limit to at least 192, which covers the observed maximum train span of 187 and development span of 167, while preserving the same source/split and reporting its increased compute separately. No CAID3 metric may enter that decision.

Variant 3 implements that prespecified change: coupled span-to-residue aggregation with `max_span=192`, retaining seed 7, the filtered files, encoder, batch size, 12 epochs, optimizer, and APS-based development selection. A 200-residue forward smoke test and the fail-closed data preflight passed. It ran in one isolated background process, PID 35744, with a 1,800-second trial limit; output/logs are under `artifacts/dfl-coupled-long-span-protected-2026-10-16/`. It completed in 1,284.172 seconds with best filtered-development APS .495796, AUROC .855176, Fmax .563504, high-precision recall 0, and IoU-0.5 region recall 0/28 at threshold .5. Peak allocated PyTorch VRAM was 664,131,072 bytes. Increasing the span ceiling improved the coupled 64-residue APS by .035088, but runtime increased 2.55-fold and the result remained .295528 below the matched SAM-like direct seed-7 APS .791324. The result is negative, not promising enough for additional seeds. No fourth variant is authorized within this round.

The full-label span ceiling was real, but eliminating it did **not** close the performance gap. The tested span heads use two local width-5 convolutions, whereas the stronger SAM-like baseline includes global self-attention; that architecture difference is a plausible next mechanism to test, not an established causal explanation. A new round must be separately documented and matched on the screened data. Do not compare the present long-span score to a baseline as a compute-matched improvement. No checkpoint from this round is promoted, and CAID3 remains unscored for model selection.
