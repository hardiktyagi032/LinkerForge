# Protected competitor-gap screen: preregistration

Before reading any new development result, screen three single-mechanism SAM-like heads: `global_context`, `dilated_local`, and `predicted_disorder_gate`. They are adaptations motivated by APOD's protein-wide context and TransDFL's local/long-range and IDR-transfer mechanisms, not faithful reimplementations. The existing matched comparator is `samlike-disorder_transfer-seed7` on the same CAID3-protected train/development/auxiliary files and frozen ESM2-35M. The new heads keep the same 3 auxiliary IDR epochs, 12 DFL epochs, optimizer, learning rate, windowing, score channel, masked APS checkpoint selection, and seed 7. For the disorder-gate arm, its IDR subhead receives auxiliary supervision; its DFL output uses only predicted disorder logits at inference.

Config: `configs/dfl_competitor_gap_round_2026-10-17.json`. Model: `src/linkerforge/models/competitor_gap_segmenter.py`. Runner: `scripts/run_caid3_protected_competitor_gap_trial.py`. Each job re-runs protected-data preflight; the queue validates frozen config/source/data hashes, has isolated outputs, one GPU job at a time, at most three architecture variants and six GPU-hours. Local CPU smoke passed for the three executable modes; external-representation and function-interaction modes refuse missing inputs and are **not** in this round.

Primary development screen: masked residue APS. Also compare AUROC, empirical recall at precision >=0.90 with >=50 TP and exact ties, region recall at IoU 0.5, the 17-protein no-positive proxy specificity, Fmax after checkpoint rescoring, runtime, VRAM and failures. A seed-7 difference is only exploratory; no variant is promoted without three-seed repetition and the previously declared <=0.02 specificity-loss guardrail. Because one additional false-positive development protein moves the proxy by 0.0588, report raw protein counts and treat this measure as low-resolution. CAID3 is already used for a frozen public retrospective; it is not opened or repeated to choose among these heads.
