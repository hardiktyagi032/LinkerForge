# DFL positive-candidate coordinate gate

The hash-checked `scripts/audit_dfl_candidate_coordinates.py` audit of `data/processed/dfl_positive_candidates_2026_10_12.jsonl` found 205 unique proteins, 256 curated positive-region records, 9,730 positive residues, and 149,488 masked/unknown residues. Every sequence and label string had matching length; every region coordinate was in range; the union of region coordinates matched the positive mask exactly; and there were no `0` negatives in this positive-only catalog. Full evidence-code counts and affected IDs are in `reports/dfl_candidate_coordinate_audit_2026-10-17.json`.

There are **21 region-overlap events** across the annotated records. Overlap is not automatically erroneous—two curated records can cover the same position—but these cases need evidence/annotation review before regions are treated as distinct training examples or counted independently. The 9,730 figure counts unique positive mask positions, not the sum of overlapping region lengths. The audit did **not** verify the current-release sequence against each historical region source, interpret ECO evidence strength, establish domain-pair function, or certify family independence. Candidate training eligibility remains gated by those checks and the separate protected-sequence component audit.
